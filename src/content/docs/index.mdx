## Getting Help

If you need support or assistance, we're always here to help! Below are the available ways to get in touch with us.

### Support Options

- [**Open a Ticket**](https://portal.evlbox.com/index.php?rp=/login) – **Login required** to submit a support ticket for direct assistance.
- [**Discord Ticket Support**](https://discord.gg/rWF4hJhbaM) – **Login required** to link your Discord account or provide your support pin from **EVLBOX Portal**.

### Support Hours
**8 AM - 10 PM GMT-7 (AZ Time)**
Please note that response times may be slower during busy periods or **US National Holidays**. Thank you for your patience!
