Add additional users to your Nitro Control Panel. Useful for community servers that have additional staff who need to be able to administer the server. 

User permissions are selectively assigned, giving you the ability to control exactly what actions your sub-user can perform. 

## Adding a Sub-User

1. Under Server Controls, choose "Users"

![users.jpg](/docs/nitro/users.jpg)

2. Click the add user button 
![image.png](/docs/nitro/image.png)

3. Enter the users email address (they will use this to sign into the Nitro Panel).  Then scroll down to check the permissions you wish to
