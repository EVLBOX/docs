# Schedule I

Schedule I is a multiplayer crime simulation where you build your empire in the gritty streets of Hyland Point. Run operations, manage your crew, and climb the underworld ladder — now with full dedicated server support through the S1 DedicatedServerMod.

[Schedule I Servers](https://evlbox.com/schedule-1-server-hosting/)

:::tip
New to Schedule I servers? Start with [How to Connect](/docs/game-servers/schedule-1/how-to-connect) to install the required client mods, then check [Server Settings](/docs/game-servers/schedule-1/server-settings) to configure your world.
:::

## Hosting Guides

[How to Connect to Your Server](/docs/game-servers/schedule-1/how-to-connect) — Install MelonLoader and the S1 client mod to connect to your dedicated server.
