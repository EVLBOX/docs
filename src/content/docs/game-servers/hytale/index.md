# **Hytale Hosting Guide**
![Hytale Game Logo](/docs/hytale-logo.png)

**Hytale** is a next-generation sandbox RPG that combines the creative freedom of block-building with the depth of a traditional role-playing game. Launched into Early Access on January 13, 2026, it is developed by Hypixel Studios—the team behind the world’s largest Minecraft server—and is designed from the ground up to be "creator-first."

With **EVLBOX Hytale Hosting**, you can easily manage your server, optimize your gameplay, and create the perfect environment for your adventure for you and your friends!
