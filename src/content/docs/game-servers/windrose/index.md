![header.jpg](/docs/windrose/header.jpg)


## Server Requirements

Pick a plan based on the size of your crew. All EVLBOX Windrose servers run on fast NVMe SSD storage, and slot counts are unlimited — only resources (RAM, CPU, disk) are capped per plan.

| Plan        | Crew size      | RAM   | CPU                                 |
|-------------|----------------|-------|-------------------------------------|
| **Sloop**   | 2–4 players    | 8 GB  | 2 cores (Ryzen 7 3800X or better)   |
| **Brig**    | 4–6 players    | 12 GB | 3 cores (Ryzen 9 5900X or better)   |
| **Frigate** | 6–8 players    | 16 GB | 4 cores (Ryzen 9 7950X, Dallas only)|

:::note
Crew-size recommendations align with the [official Windrose dedicated server guide](https://playwindrose.com/dedicated-server-guide/#wsg-faq-sysreq), which lists 8 GB / 2 players, 12 GB / 4 players, 16 GB / 10 players.
:::
