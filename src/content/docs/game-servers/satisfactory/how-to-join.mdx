#  Joining your Server

:::caution[PS5 and Xbox players can't join]
Dedicated servers are PC-only, so console players can't join your server, even with Satisfactory's new crossplay. See [1.2 and Crossplay](/docs/game-servers/satisfactory/satisfactory-1).
:::

1. Find your address and Primary port on the **Network** tab in the Nitro Panel. See [first time setup](/docs/game-servers/satisfactory/first-time-setup) if you need help.
2. In Satisfactory, open the **Server Manager** and click **Add Server**.
3. Enter your address and Primary port, then click **Confirm**.

   ![Server Manager with an address and port entered](/docs/satisfactory/join_server.png)

4. When the Server Certificate Warning appears, click **Confirm**.

   ![certificate_warning.png](/docs/satisfactory/certificate_warning.png)

5. If your server is password-protected, enter the password.

   ![authenticate.png](/docs/satisfactory/authenticate.png)

## Can't join?

- **The game just updated:** restart your server from the Nitro Panel. It installs updates when it starts, as long as **Automatic Updates** is on.
- **You're on the experimental branch:** your game and your server need to be on the same branch. See [Switch to Experimental](/docs/game-servers/satisfactory/switch-to-experimental).
- **You time out while joining a big or modded save:** raise **Join Timeout** on the **Startup** tab and restart your server.
