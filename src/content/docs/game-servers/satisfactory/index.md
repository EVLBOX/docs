# **Satisfactory Hosting with EVLBOX**
![Satisfactory Game Logo](/docs/satisfactory/satisfactory_logo.webp)

**Satisfactory** is an open-world, first-person game that combines **factory construction** with exploration and combat. Whether you're playing solo or with friends, dive into an **alien planet**, build massive factories, and master the art of **conveyor belt engineering**!

With **EVLBOX Satisfactory Hosting**, you can easily manage your server, optimize your gameplay, and create the perfect environment for your factory-building adventure.

:::caution
Dedicated servers are PC-only. PS5 and Xbox players can't join, even with Satisfactory's new crossplay. See [1.2 and Crossplay](/docs/game-servers/satisfactory/satisfactory-1).
:::

## **Updates**

**11/4/2026**
**Patch 1.2.5.0** adds crossplay between PC and console for player-hosted sessions. It doesn't work on dedicated servers, so PS5 and Xbox players can't join your EVLBOX server. Restart your server to install the patch.

**6/2/2026**
**Satisfactory 1.2** is now on the stable branch. You don't need the experimental branch to play it.
