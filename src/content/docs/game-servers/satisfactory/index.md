# **Satisfactory Hosting with EVLBOX**
![Satisfactory Game Logo](/docs/satisfactory/satisfactory_logo.webp)

**Satisfactory** is an open-world, first-person game that combines **factory construction** with exploration and combat. Whether you’re playing solo or with friends, dive into an **alien planet**, build massive factories, and master the art of **conveyor belt engineering**!

With **EVLBOX Satisfactory Hosting**, you can easily manage your server, optimize your gameplay, and create the perfect environment for your factory-building adventure.

---

## **Hosting Guides**

Looking to get started or need help managing your **Satisfactory server**? Check out our comprehensive guides below:

- [**Setting Up a Satisfactory Server**](https://wiki.evlbox.com/satisfactory/first-time-setup) – Step-by-step instructions to get your **Satisfactory server** up and running for the first time.
  
- [**Copy Your Singleplayer Save to Your Server**](https://wiki.evlbox.com/satisfactory/upload-singleplayer-save) – Learn how to upload your **singleplayer or multiplayer saves** to your **EVLBOX server**.
  
- [**How to Join Your Satisfactory Server**](https://wiki.evlbox.com/satisfactory/how-to-join) – A guide on how to connect to your **Satisfactory server** from within the game.
  
- [**How to Manage Saves**](https://wiki.evlbox.com/satisfactory/manage-saves) – Tips on **downloading** and **managing multiple saves** on your **Satisfactory server**.

- [**How to Unclaim a Satisfactory Server**](https://wiki.evlbox.com/satisfactory/unclaim-server) – Need to reset your ownership or forgot your admin password? We’ve got you covered!

- [**Satisfactory 1.1 Updates**](https://wiki.evlbox.com/satisfactory/satisfactory-1) – Everything you need to know about updating, connecting, and known issues with **Satisfactory 1.0**.

- [**Satisfactory 1.0 Mod Setup**](https://wiki.evlbox.com/satisfactory/modsetup) – Learn how to **install mods** and **connect your server** to use **custom mods**.

- [**Switch to Experimental Branch**](https://wiki.evlbox.com/satisfactory/switch-to-experimental) – Guide to switching your server to the **experimental branch** (required for new content in **Satisfactory 1.1**).
